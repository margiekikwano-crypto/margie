Who We Are

Bugema University Vocational School is a dedicated centre of excellence under Bugema University, committed to equipping learners with practical, industry-relevant skills that transform communities and inspire service. Rooted in the University's Seventh-day Adventist heritage since 1948, we blend academic rigor with hands-on training to empower youth and adults for meaningful employment, entrepreneurship, and leadership.

📍 Located at Bugema, Luwero District(32Km Gayaza-Zirobwe Rd), Uganda

OUR HISTORY

1948 • THE FOUNDATION

Bugema University is established as a Seventh-day Adventist centre for Christian education and service in Luwero, Uganda.

1950s-1970s • ACCREDITATION & EXPANSION

First buildings constructed -classrooms,offices and dining hall with grades 1-5 teacher training.

1997 • VOCATIONAL SCHOOL LAUNCHED

Recieved charter as Bugema University - Vocational School retained as a distinct unit under the University
