Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Bugema Vocational Institute

What are the admission requirements?

Minimum P.7 certificate for short courses and S.4 for diploma programs. Come with national ID, 2 passport photos. No interview for most courses.

Can I apply online?

Yes. Fill our online application form, you will receive SMS confirmation within 24hrs and your data is securely stored in our database.

How much is tuition? Can I pay in installments?

Fees from UGX 450,000 per semester depending on course. We allow 2 installments: 60% on reporting, 40% before exams. No extra charges.

Is there bursary?

Yes, partial bursaries for needy students and Adventist members. Apply through Principal's office with LC1 letter.

Which courses do you offer?

Plumbing, Electrical Installation, Tailoring & Fashion, Catering, Computer, Building & Construction, Hairdressing. All certified by DIT & UBTEB.

How long are the courses?

Short courses 3-6 months, Certificate 1 year, Diploma 2 years. All include industrial training.

Do you have hostel?

Yes, separate hostels for boys and girls inside campus, with security and matron. UGX 250,000 per semester including meals.
