How much are the fees & can I pay in installments?
+
Fees is UGX 600k - 800k per semester. YES you can pay in installments: 50% to start, rest later. No hidden charges. Hostel is separate UGX 400k. We give receipt for every payment.
🎓
What do I need to join? Do you need high grades?
+
No high grades needed. We accept P7, S4, S6. Come with photocopy of results + National ID + 2 passport photos. If you can read and write English, you qualify.
📅
When is next intake & how long is course?
+
Next intake: January 2026. 1 Year (Tailoring, Computer, Catering, Hair) and 2 Years (Electrical, Carpentry, Welding, Plumbing). Certificate on graduation day.
🛡️
Is hostel safe for my daughter?
+
100% safe. Christian university, fenced, 24hr security, separate hostels for boys/girls, Matron & Warden inside, curfew 9pm. Parents can visit anytime. This is why many parents choose us.
🔧
Do you give practical tools or just theory?
+
80% practical. We have: 40 sewing machines, electrical lab, carpentry workshop with timber, welding machines, 50 computers, modern kitchen. You touch tools from week 1.
📜
Which certificate will I get? Will I get job?
+
Bugema University Certificate + DIT Certificate (National). 8 out of 10 students start earning within 6 months - either job or own business. We add entrepreneurship + we link you to companies.
